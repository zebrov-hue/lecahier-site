A parent’s project

Practice at home what your child is learning in class.

Every school day, your child does a short practice session on the subjects covered in class (Grades 1 to 6).

If you wish, you upload the weekly class plan: Le Cahier then chooses exercises based on what is being taught and on upcoming spelling tests and other tests. In every case, it adapts to your child’s answers: a mistake on a topic covered in class brings it back in later sessions, and what’s mastered comes back less and less often.

Real exercises, not a planner. Here’s what a session looks like.

You don’t have to figure out where your child is struggling.

With a worksheet, you check the answers, figure out where your child is stuck and look for an explanation for every mistake. I wanted Le Cahier to do that work.

An example. A child misplaces 7/10 on a number line. A hint comes first, with a second try; then the solution is explained. In the following sessions, if the concept has been covered in class, it comes back until it has been answered correctly twice, on two different days. After that, it only returns now and then, to check that it has stuck.

You can see how your child is doing, subject by subject. There’s nothing for you to set up.

An example week

Weekly class plan: a math test on Thursday and a spelling test on Friday. Here’s what Le Cahier prepares.

Monday

  • Math: fraction questions (test on Thursday)
  • Spelling: this week’s words

Tuesday

  • Math: a full word problem
  • Spelling: this week’s words

Wednesday

  • Math: arithmetic drills and a final round of fraction questions
  • Review: the concept missed on Tuesday comes back, with an explanation

Thursday

  • Spelling: a practice test on this week’s words
  • Math: a last round of fraction questions

Friday

  • In class: spelling test
  • Le Cahier: a short session reviewing the week

How does it work?

1

You create an account

All you need is an email address and a password. For your child, just a first name.

2

You can upload the class plan

If you have the class plan the school sends you, you can add it; a photo, screenshot or PDF (up to 3 pages) is enough. No plan this week? Le Cahier still adapts to your child’s answers. Got a marked test back? Add it too: a concept with mistakes counts as seen in class, so it comes back in the sessions.

3

Your child does today’s session

One button: “Ma séance du jour” (today’s session). It’s shorter on busy weeks and longer before a test. Spelling words are read aloud. If your child wants more, an extra session is ready. On weekends, a session is offered, never required.

4

You track your child’s progress

Each week, at a glance: what Le Cahier is working on, what’s left to do on paper or in class, and the dates to remember. A first progress overview for each subject is available after about ten days.

What sets it apart, and what it isn’t

What sets it apart

Exercises built on the Québec Education Program, the official curriculum of Québec’s Ministère de l’Éducation, then matched to what’s happening in your child’s class: the weekly class plan, spelling tests and other tests.

After a mistake: a hint and a second try, then the explanation. If the concept has been covered in class, it then comes back at spaced intervals.

No points, no rankings, no streaks to keep up.

What it isn’t

A tutor or a replacement for the teacher.

A homework planner: these exercises are extra practice, on top of homework.

One more app to keep track of: no reminder is sent without your agreement, nothing to follow up on.

Your data

Only you, the parent, can read your child’s answers, written work, interests and first name. The answers and written work your child writes in Le Cahier are never sent to an external service. Your questions

How much time does it take?

A session takes up to about 20 minutes. It’s shorter on busy weeks, and some days there’s nothing to do.

What is the weekly class plan for?

It keeps Le Cahier in step with the class: what’s being taught this week, spelling tests and upcoming tests. If it refers to pages in a common textbook, Le Cahier works out the concept when it can. Without a plan, Le Cahier still adapts to your child’s answers, but it won’t know what the class is working on. A concept counts as “covered in class” when it is named in a class plan, when a marked test shows mistakes on it, or when your child gets it right alone on two different days. Until a concept is in one of those cases, a mistake does not bring it back.

What is a marked test for?

It shows what was hard in class. You add a photo or a PDF (1 to 6 pages), and Le Cahier brings back the concepts where there were mistakes. The top of each page (name, mark) is hidden automatically before it is read, and you can hide other areas. Only the concepts and the date are kept: not the name, the mark or the photo.

Does it follow the school curriculum?

Yes. The exercises are built on the official curriculum of Québec’s Ministère de l’Éducation, and the weekly class plan matches them to your child’s week.

Website privacy policy

This policy covers only the lecahier.ca website and its form. The app has its own privacy policy, which you can read before creating an account.

What I collect

Your first name, your email, your child’s school grade and your agreement that I write to you. Nothing else: no child’s name, no school, no results.

Why

Only to write to you about Le Cahier and its launch. No sale of your information, no newsletter without your separate agreement. Without your consent, this site uses no tracking cookies or advertising pixel.

Audience measurement (optional)

Only with your consent, I use the Meta pixel (Facebook and Instagram) to know whether my posts bring people to this site and how many fill in the form. Meta then receives information about your visit: IP address, device and browser, pages viewed, and the fact that the form was submitted. It does not receive your first name, your email or your child’s grade. This information may be processed outside Quebec.

Automated analysis, in the app

To read the photo of the class plan or of a marked test, the app sends it to an external service located outside Quebec. On a test, the top of the page (name, mark) is hidden before it is sent. Any names visible on the plan are removed from the result before anything is saved, and the photo is not kept. The answers and written work your child writes in Le Cahier are never sent to an external service. A photo or PDF of a marked test may contain your child’s handwriting; the top of the page is hidden before it is sent. The details are in the app’s policy.

Who has access

Only me, as the person in charge of this site. Form submissions go through the site’s host, CanSpace, whose servers are located in Beauharnois, Québec.

How long

12 months after our last exchange. After that, your details are deleted. You can also ask for their deletion at any time.
