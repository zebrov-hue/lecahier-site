A parent’s project

Practice at home what your child learns in class.

Every day, your child does a short exercise session: math, dictation, reading, writing, science, social studies.

Each week, you show the class work plan. Le Cahier picks exercises based on what is being taught, the upcoming dictations and tests, then adapts to your child’s answers: what was mastered isn’t repeated, what was missed comes back.

How does it work?

1

You create the account

An email and a password. For your child, just a first name.

2

You show the class plan

Each week, you add the work plan the school sends you: a photo or a screenshot is enough.

3

Your child does the session

One button: “Ma séance du jour” (today’s session). It is shorter on busy weeks, longer before a test. On weekends, nothing is required.

4

You follow their progress

Most days: “Nothing to do on your side today.” A first picture by subject appears after about ten days.

What sets it apart, and what it isn’t

What sets it apart

Exercises built from the official curriculum of Quebec’s Ministère de l’Éducation, then matched to your child’s class: work plan, dictations, tests.

After a mistake: an explanation, then the concept comes back at spaced intervals.

No points, no rankings, no streak to maintain.

What it isn’t

A tutor, or a replacement for the teacher.

A homework planner: these are exercises on top of homework.

One more app to keep an eye on: no notifications, nothing to follow up.

Your questions

How much time should I plan for?

A session is short. It is even shorter on busy weeks, a little longer before a test, and some days there is nothing to do.

What is the class work plan for?

It is what aligns Le Cahier with the school: what is taught this week, the dictations, the upcoming tests. Without it, Le Cahier still works from your child’s answers, but it doesn’t know what the class is working on.

Does it follow the school curriculum?

Yes. The exercises are built from the official curriculum of the Ministère de l’Éducation, and the class plan matches them to your child’s week.

Can I stop whenever I want?

Yes. You can delete your child’s cahier or your account, and everything attached to it is erased.

Privacy on this site

This policy covers only the lecahier.ca website and its form. The app has its own privacy policy, which you can read before creating an account.

What I collect

Your first name, your email, your child’s school grade and your agreement that I write to you. Nothing else: no child’s name, no school, no results.

Why

Only to write to you about Le Cahier and its launch. No sale of your information, no newsletter without your separate agreement. Without your consent, this site uses no tracking cookies or advertising pixel.

Audience measurement (optional)

Only with your consent, I use the Meta pixel (Facebook and Instagram) to know whether my posts bring people to this site and how many fill in the form. Meta then receives information about your visit: IP address, device and browser, pages viewed, and the fact that the form was submitted. It does not receive your first name, your email or your child’s grade. This information may be processed outside Quebec.

Automated analysis, in the app

To correct a text, read the photo of the plan or explain an exercise differently, the app sends the content, without first name or surname, to an external service that may be located outside Quebec. The details are in the app’s policy.

Who has access

Me, as the person responsible. Messages are received by the site’s host, CanSpace, whose servers are located in Beauharnois (Quebec).

How long

12 months after our last exchange. After that, your details are deleted. You can also ask for their deletion at any time.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information, or withdraw your consent, by writing to najm@lecahier.ca. Person responsible for the protection of personal information: Najm Jaouadi.
